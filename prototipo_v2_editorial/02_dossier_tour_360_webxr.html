ARCHITECTURAL MONOGRAPH

Unapologetic spatial abundance where negative space operates as an active luxury material.

Conceived as a masterwork of brutalist restraint, the Mansão Brutalista Villa Imperial commands an elevated promontory in Santo Antônio de Jesus, Bahia. The structure marries raw, board-formed architectural concrete with the honed organic warmth of travertine limestone.

Monumental floor-to-ceiling glass planes retract effortlessly, dissolving the boundary between interior sanctuaries and the wild Bahian canopy. Every sightline is carefully curated to frame panoramic vistas, establishing an architectural dialogue between monumental geometry and natural topography.
